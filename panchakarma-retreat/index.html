About the retreat

Ayurveda, received personally

Step away from the demands of daily life and spend 15 days focused on your health, balance and renewal at Vaidyaratnam in Thrissur, Kerala.

Each participant receives an individualized Ayurvedic consultation and a treatment plan designed around their constitution, current health concerns, strength and needs. Experienced Ayurvedic physicians and therapists guide and monitor your care throughout.

To be clear about what this isThis is not an academic program or classroom immersion. The focus is on giving your body and mind the time, environment, treatments and nourishment needed for a meaningful restorative experience.

Questions

Good to know before you go

Is this an academic course?

No. It is a personal wellness retreat centered on receiving Panchakarma treatment, not a classroom or study program.

What does the price cover?

Airport pickup and drop-off at Kochi, accommodation, meals, consultations, Panchakarma treatments, scheduled wellness activities, local transport and tourism. Flights, visa fees and personal expenses are extra.

Will my treatment be personalized?

Yes. Every guest gets an individual consultation and plan, with daily physician follow-up and adjustments.

How do I secure a seat?

Complete the reservation form and pay a $200 deposit. With only 20 places, seats are confirmed in order of deposit.
