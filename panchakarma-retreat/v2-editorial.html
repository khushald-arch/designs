Ayurveda, received personally

Step away from the demands of daily life and spend 15 days focused on your health, balance and renewal at Vaidyaratnam in Thrissur, Kerala.

Each participant receives an individualized Ayurvedic consultation and a treatment plan built around their constitution, health concerns, strength and needs.

Experienced Ayurvedic physicians and therapists guide and monitor your care throughout, with daily follow-up and adjustments.

The group is limited to 20 participants so that your care stays personal.

“This is not an academic program or classroom immersion. It is about receiving Ayurveda personally.”
