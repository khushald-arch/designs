Overview

Ayurveda, received personally

Step away from the demands of daily life and spend 15 days focused on your health, balance and renewal at Vaidyaratnam in Thrissur, Kerala, in partnership with the California College of Ayurveda.

This retreat is centered on your personal Panchakarma experience. Each participant receives an individualized Ayurvedic consultation and a treatment plan designed around their constitution, current health concerns, strength and needs. Throughout the retreat, experienced Ayurvedic physicians and therapists guide and monitor your care.

We want to be clear about what this is. This is not an academic program or classroom immersion. The focus is on receiving Ayurveda personally: giving your body and mind the time, environment, treatments, nourishment and support needed for a meaningful restorative experience.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this Panchakarma Retreat for?

Adults seeking a personalized Ayurvedic wellness experience — for stress reduction, healthy aging, rejuvenation, overall well-being, or supportive Ayurvedic care alongside management of chronic health concerns.

When and where is the retreat?

February 13–28, 2027, at Vaidyaratnam in Thrissur, Kerala, India.

What is included in the $3,000 retreat fee?

Kochi airport pickup and drop-off, accommodation, vegetarian meals, Ayurvedic consultations, personalized Panchakarma treatments, scheduled wellness activities, local transportation, and selected cultural/tourism experiences.

What is not included?

Round-trip airfare to Kochi, visa fees, travel insurance, personal expenses, and any additional services outside the retreat program.

Will everyone receive the same Panchakarma treatments?

No. Each participant receives a personalized consultation with Vaidyaratnam physicians, and treatments are selected according to individual constitution, health status and needs.

What other wellness activities are included?

Yoga, Marma therapy, physiotherapy, traditional Kerala therapies, herbal support, therapeutic diet, relaxation practices and cultural experiences, as appropriate and advised by the physicians.

How do I reserve my place, and will you help with visas?

Only 20 spaces are available. Reserve with a $200 deposit. Participants obtain their own Indian visa, but CCA/Vaidyaratnam can provide supporting invitation letters when needed.

Will I have internet access, and can I work during the retreat?

Yes, internet is available. We strongly encourage minimizing work so you can fully benefit from the treatments, rest and reflection.

Can children participate?

Panchakarma is not automatically appropriate for children. Families should contact us in advance so suitability, accommodation and supervision can be confirmed before registration.
